& responsibilities.

Pharmionex publishes appointments only after each person has agreed to serve and their name, affiliation, expertise, and disclosures are verified. Operational functions are listed separately from appointed board members.

Reviewers are invited for individual manuscripts and are not standing board members unless separately appointed.

At launch, one person may cover compatible administrative functions, but editorial decisions must be reassigned when a conflict exists. Appeals and concerns about the Editor-in-Chief need independent handling. Do not publish a person's name, affiliation, credentials, or role without their agreement.

Governance

Editorial accountability

Editorial independence

Decisions should follow published criteria and remain independent of commercial, personal, or institutional pressure.

Conflict management

Editors and reviewers disclose relevant interests and recuse where impartiality could reasonably be questioned.

Corrections to the record

Concerns are assessed and documented; corrections, expressions of concern, or retractions are published when warranted.
