Digital preservation

Current status and interim procedure

Current status: The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. That hosting is not claimed as a long-term preservation service. The journal has not confirmed participation in an external preservation archive.

Before an article is released: the production editor should save the final article HTML and PDF with the website source, create a dated repository export and a separate access-restricted copy of the published files, then record the copy and a successful open/restore check in the private Publication Checklist. This is an interim recovery measure; it is not a claim of certified or long-term preservation.

Next governance step: the journal should select and verify a durable external archive before claiming archival coverage. Until enrolment and deposits are confirmed, the preservation-service field remains pending. Corrections and retractions must be linked to the original article record; preserve its history under the published-record policy.

The current automated backup copies the private tracker spreadsheet only. It does not back up article files or the website. Editors must use the private SECURITY_RECOVERY_CHECKLIST.txt in the complete journal package to assign owners and record recovery checks.
