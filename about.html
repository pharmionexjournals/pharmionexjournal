About the journal

Knowledge with purpose.
Evidence with integrity.

Pharmionex Research Journal (PHARMIONEX) is an independent scholarly publishing project focused on pharmaceutical and allied health sciences. Its publishing framework is being established; public claims reflect only verified status.

Aims & scope

Connecting research and practice

The journal aims to provide a platform for original research, reviews, case reports and brief scientific communications in pharmaceutical sciences and related fields.

Areas of interest include pharmaceutics and drug delivery, pharmacology and toxicology, pharmaceutical analysis and chemistry, pharmacognosy, clinical and hospital pharmacy, pharmacovigilance, regulatory science, biotechnology, pharmaceutical technology and pharmacy education.

New journal: Pharmionex has not yet established a publication track record or earned external indexing or recognition. ISSN and DOI registration are pending; no indexing is claimed.
