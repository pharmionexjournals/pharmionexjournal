About the journal

Knowledge with purpose.
Evidence with integrity.

Pharmionex Research Journal (PHARMIONEX) is an independent scholarly publishing project focused on sharing research in pharmaceutical and allied health sciences through a clear, accessible and responsible publication workflow.

Aims & scope

Connecting research and practice

The journal aims to provide a platform for original research, review articles, case reports and brief scientific communications that contribute to pharmaceutical knowledge, healthcare practice and scientific methods.

Areas of interest include pharmaceutics and drug delivery, pharmacology and toxicology, pharmaceutical analysis and chemistry, pharmacognosy, clinical and hospital pharmacy, pharmacovigilance, regulatory science, biotechnology, pharmaceutical technology and pharmacy education.

Launch-status note: The journal is currently being prepared. Claims about ISSN, DOI registration, indexing, publication frequency, peer-review completion or formal recognition should be made only after the relevant status is actually confirmed.

What we value

Responsible scholarly communication

Research integrity

Authors should report methods and results accurately, preserve research records and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Fair assessment

Editorial decisions should consider scope, originality, methodological quality, clarity, ethical compliance and relevance.

Corrections & accountability

The journal should provide routes for corrections, complaints, appeals and concerns about research or publication conduct.
