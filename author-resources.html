Reporting guideline matrix
Choose the checklist that fits your study
Use the current official checklist for the design and cite the guideline in the manuscript. Checklist names below are signposts, not endorsements or a substitute for the full guideline.
Study / article typeCommon reporting resourceSubmission item
Randomized trialCONSORTCompleted checklist and participant flow diagram; trial registration where applicable
Systematic review / meta-analysisPRISMACompleted checklist, search strategy and study flow diagram
Observational studySTROBECompleted checklist; identify cohort, case-control or cross-sectional design
Case reportCARECompleted checklist and publication consent
Diagnostic accuracy studySTARDCompleted checklist and participant flow, as applicable
Animal researchARRIVECompleted checklist and ethics approval details
Qualitative researchCOREQ or SRQRChecklist matching interview/focus-group or broader qualitative design
Quality improvementSQUIRECompleted checklist and context/intervention description
Economic evaluationCHEERSCompleted checklist and model/data details
ProtocolSPIRIT (trial) or PRISMA-P (review)Protocol checklist; registration details if applicable
Consult the guideline publisher for current versions and extensions relevant to your study. If no guideline fits, explain the reporting approach used. The journal does not imply affiliation with these guideline organizations.

Case seriesDescribe cases, outcomes, limitations, and written publication consent where needed~3,000 words; abstract 150–250
Pharmacy practice / education / pharmacovigilanceUse the design-appropriate structure and reporting checklist; describe setting and data source~4,000 words; abstract 150–300
Commentary / perspective / letterFocused, evidence-based argument; disclose relevant interests and sources~1,500 words; editor may set format
Word counts are planning ranges, not automatic rejection thresholds. Editors may consider other well-designed work directly related to pharmaceutical sciences and allied fields; contact the office before submitting a format not listed here.


Revision response
How to respond to reviewers
  1. Thank the editor and reviewers; quote or identify each comment separately.
  2. Reply point by point, indicating the exact change and page/line location in the revised manuscript.
  3. Where you disagree, explain the reasoning respectfully and support it with evidence or citations.
  4. Submit a clean manuscript and a tracked/marked version if requested; confirm all authors approved the revision.


Reference examples
Numbered biomedical references
Journal article: Author AA, Author BB. Article title. Abbreviated Journal Title. Year;volume(issue):pages. doi:10.xxxx/xxxxx.
Book: Author AA. Book title. 2nd ed. City: Publisher; Year.
Web resource: Organization. Page title [Internet]. Place: Publisher; year [cited YYYY Mon DD]. Available from: URL
Number references by first citation; reuse the original number for repeat citations. Verify author names, title, year, pages and DOI against the source. Use a DOI URL only when verified.
