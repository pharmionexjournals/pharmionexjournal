🎯 Aims & Scope

The Journal publishes high-quality peer-reviewed original research articles, systematic reviews, short communications, and technical methodologies. The scope encompasses pharmaceutical sciences, analytical chemistry, biotechnology, molecular medicine, high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), formulation science, clinical studies, and computational machine learning applications in biomedical sciences.

📋 Author Submission Guidelines

  • Structure: Original research articles must follow the IMRAD format (Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion).
  • Abstract: Must not exceed 300 words and should provide clear context, methods, key findings, and scientific significance.
  • Keywords: 4 to 6 indexing terms conforming to MeSH (Medical Subject Headings).
  • File Formats: Manuscripts are accepted in PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), or LaTeX (.zip).
  • Ethics: All submissions must include funding declarations, conflict of interest disclosures, and data availability statements.
