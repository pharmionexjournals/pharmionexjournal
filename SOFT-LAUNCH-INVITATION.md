# Soft launch: invitation texts

Use these for the first issue, sent personally to 5-10 people you know. Do not post a public call yet.

## Personal email to prospective authors

**Subject:** Invitation to contribute to the inaugural issue of Pharmionex Journal

Dear Dr. [Name],

I am the founding editor of Pharmionex Journal, a new open-access, peer-reviewed journal for pharmaceutical, biomedical and clinical research. We are preparing our first issue (Volume 1, Issue 1) and I am inviting a small number of colleagues to submit.

A few honest points so you can decide:
- It is a **new journal**. It has no ISSN or indexing yet, and I can't promise it will count toward any formal academic evaluation. Please check with your institution first.
- There are **no charges** for authors or readers, and articles are published under CC BY 4.0.
- Every manuscript goes through desk screening, a similarity check and **double-blind review by two external reviewers**. Our target for a first decision is about 4-6 weeks, and it may be longer while we are small.
- You keep your copyright.

If you have a manuscript that fits, you can submit here: https://pharmionexjournals.github.io/pharmionexjournal/ (use "Submit Online"). You will get an email with a Tracking ID. Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are linked at the bottom of the site.

If the timing doesn't suit you, no problem at all. I would also welcome your suggestions of reviewers in your area.

Kind regards,
Vivek Sharma
Editor-in-Chief, Pharmionex Journal
pharmionex.journal@gmail.com

## Short message (WhatsApp / LinkedIn, one-to-one)

Hi [Name], I'm launching Pharmionex Journal, a new free open-access journal in pharma sciences, and I'm inviting a few colleagues to send papers for the first issue. It's brand new, with no ISSN yet, so no pressure. If you have something suitable, details are at https://pharmionexjournals.github.io/pharmionexjournal/

## Public call for papers (hold until ready)
Hold the public call until:
- the editorial board (5-8 people) is listed,
- the ISSN has been issued,
- at least one issue's worth of papers has been published,
- and reviewers and similarity checking are confirmed.
When ready, switch the site wording back from "Inaugural Issue: Invited Submissions" to "Call for Papers" in `index.html` and `script.js`.
