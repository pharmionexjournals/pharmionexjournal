# Reviewer recruitment

## 1. Invitation to join the reviewer pool (send before launch)

**Subject:** Invitation to join the reviewer pool, Pharmionex Journal

Dear Dr. [Name],

I am the Editor-in-Chief of Pharmionex Journal, a new open-access journal covering pharmaceutical, biomedical and clinical research. We are preparing our inaugural issue and are building a small pool of external reviewers. Your work on [specific topic or paper] is close to the manuscripts we expect to receive.

If you agree, we would occasionally send you an anonymised manuscript in your area. Each review is double-blind, and we would ask for your report within about three weeks. You can decline any manuscript, and we will not send more than [number] per year without asking.

We are a new journal. We do not have an ISSN or indexing yet, and there is no payment for reviewing. I can offer a reviewer acknowledgement letter and a listing in the journal's reviewer acknowledgements (only if you agree).

You can see how the journal works here: https://pharmionexjournals.github.io/pharmionexjournal/

If you are willing, please reply "Yes" with your preferred email, institution and two or three areas of expertise. If you would rather not, no reply is needed.

With thanks,
Vivek Sharma
Editor-in-Chief, Pharmionex Journal
pharmionex.journal@gmail.com

## 3. Review request (what the Sheet's "Send reviewer invitation" menu sends)
The script already sends only the title, type, track, abstract and keywords, with no author details. Before sending, check the abstract itself for names or institutions.

## 4. Reviewer checklist to send with the manuscript
- Is the question clear and the work original for this field?
- Are methods described well enough to repeat, and is validation (for example ICH Q2) adequate?
- Do the data support the conclusions? Are statistics appropriate?
- Are ethics, funding and conflict-of-interest statements present?
- Recommendation: accept / minor revision / major revision / reject, with comments for the author and (optionally) confidential comments for the editor.
- Please do not upload the manuscript to AI tools, and do not try to identify the authors.
