1. Introduction Core Section

Provide the scientific background, elucidate the current formulation/therapeutic challenges, and state the objective and hypothesis of this investigation.

2. Materials & Methods Core Section

Detail all reagents, raw materials, analytical instruments, chromatographic parameters, formulation steps, and animal ethics / clinical protocols to guarantee experimental reproducibility.

3. Results Core Section

Present experimental findings clearly with statistical validation (mean ± SD, p-values), chromatographic curves, and dissolution kinetic release profiles.

4. Discussion Core Section

Correlate observed outcomes with peer-reviewed pharmaceutical literature, explain underlying physicochemical mechanisms, and state study limitations.

5. Conclusion Core Section

Conclude with concise statements summarizing the novelty, therapeutic significance, and proposed future research trajectory.
