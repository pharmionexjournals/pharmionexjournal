🔬 Comprehensive Scope Across All Pharmaceutical Disciplines

Pharmionex Journal welcomes high-quality submissions spanning the entire spectrum of pharmaceutical science and healthcare:

1. Pharmaceutics & Drug Delivery: Lipid nanoparticles (SLN, NLC), liposomes, SNEDDS, polymeric micelles, transdermal, ocular, pulmonary, and targeted anticancer delivery systems.
2. Pharmaceutical Analysis & QA: Stability-indicating HPLC/UPLC, LC-MS/MS, capillary electrophoresis, forced degradation, impurity profiling under ICH Q2(R1) validation.
3. Industrial Pharmacy & GMP: Hard gelatin capsules, solid oral dosage forms, WHO Schedule M compliance, 21 CFR Part 11 computerized validation, cleanroom engineering.
4. Pharmacology & Pharmacokinetics: Preclinical animal models (IAEC), Level A IVIVC correlation, bioequivalence, compartmental PK/PD deconvolution, toxicokinetics.
5. Medicinal Chemistry & Discovery: Computer-aided drug design (CADD), molecular docking, QSAR modeling, synthesis and bio-evaluation of novel therapeutic heterocycles.
6. Pharmacognosy & Phytomedicine: Herbal drug standardization, bioactive phytoconstituents isolation, chromatographic fingerprinting, and phytotherapeutic mechanisms.
7. Clinical Pharmacy & Pharmacovigilance: Adverse drug reactions (ADR) causality assessment, drug interactions, therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), hospital practice.
8. Biopharmaceutics & Biotechnology: Monoclonal antibodies, charge heterogeneity, biosimilars, peptide therapeutics, recombinant protein characterization.

    📖 Instructions for Authors & Manuscript Guidelines

    Comprehensive standards for manuscript preparation, reporting frameworks, reference styles, and ethical requirements.

    🏛️ Publication Standards

    Pharmionex Journal is a new journal. Manuscripts must meet the following requirements.

    • Peer Review: Every manuscript undergoes double-blind review by at least two independent subject experts before a decision.
    • Originality & Plagiarism: Work must be original and unpublished. Manuscripts are screened for similarity; those above 10% may be returned to the authors before peer review.
    • Publication Ethics: COPE and ICMJE standards apply to authorship, conflicts of interest, data integrity, corrections and retractions. Fabrication, falsification, duplicate submission and salami slicing are not accepted.
    • Authorship & Identity: Full names, affiliations, corresponding-author email and ORCID iD are required for each author. Author contributions must be stated.
    • Research Ethics: IAEC / IEC approval, informed consent and CTRI registration (where applicable) must be reported in the manuscript.
    • Transparency: Funding, conflict of interest and data availability statements are mandatory. Use of AI tools must be disclosed; AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
    • Referencing: Consistent Vancouver style, with DOIs. Avoid citations to predatory or unverifiable sources.
    • Open Access & Licence: Articles are published under CC BY 4.0 with no author fees.

    Note: This is a new journal. It has no ISSN or indexing yet and makes no claim about UGC listing or academic credit; check with your institution how it treats publications in new journals. See the Editorial Board & Policies page for journal details and policies.

    🧾 Standard Manuscript Structure

    1. Title page: Title, running title, all authors with affiliations and ORCID iDs, corresponding author email.
    2. Abstract & Keywords: Structured abstract within the limit for the article type; 4 to 6 keywords.
    3. Main text: Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion (or the structure for your article type).
    4. Back matter: Acknowledgements, Author Contributions (CRediT roles), Funding, Conflict of Interest, Ethics Statement, Data Availability.
    5. References: Numbered in order of citation, Vancouver style, with DOIs.
    6. Tables, figures & supplementary files: Each cited in the text with a numbered title and legend.

    📐 Manuscript Formatting

    • File Formats: Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), or LaTeX archive (.zip).
    • Typography: 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial, double-spaced with continuous line numbers.
    • Title Page: Running title, full author names, institutional affiliations, and corresponding author email.
    • Keywords: 4 to 6 terms chosen from Medical Subject Headings (MeSH).
    • Author Details: ORCID iD for every author; contributions stated using CRediT roles.
    • Declarations: Funding, conflict of interest, ethics and data availability statements included in the manuscript.

    🖼️ Figures & Artwork (300 DPI)

    • Resolution: Minimum 300 DPI for photographs and halftones; 600 DPI for line graphs.
    • Formats: TIFF, PNG, EPS, or high-resolution PDF.
    • Labeling: 8–10 pt sans-serif font (Arial/Helvetica); clearly defined axes and units.
    • Chromatograms: Clear baseline, labeled retention times, and solvent peak annotations.

    📚 Reference Citations (Vancouver Style)

    • Citation in Text: Numbered consecutively in order of appearance using square brackets [1] or superscripts.
    • Journal Article Example: Sharma V, Patel RK. Formulation of lipid nanocarriers. Pharmionex J. 2026;1(1):1-16.
    • Pharmacopoeial Reference: United States Pharmacopeia. USP 46–NF 41. Rockville, MD; 2023.
    • DOI Requirement: Include the DOI for each cited reference where available.

    ☑️ 7. References: Formatted in standard Vancouver style with DOIs.
    ☑️ 8. Data Access: Raw chromatographic / spectral datasets available in supplementary files.
    ☑️ 9. ORCID & Contributions: ORCID iD and CRediT author contributions provided for all authors.
    ☑️ 10. Structure: Abstract, keywords, main text, back matter and references follow the standard manuscript structure.
    ☑️ 11. AI Disclosure: Any use of AI tools declared; no AI tool listed as an author.
    ☑️ 12. Journal Quality: All cited sources are verifiable; no citations to predatory journals.

    🔄 Double-Blind Peer Review Workflow

    1. Scope & Similarity Check

    Similarity screening (manuscripts above 10% may be returned) and editorial fit review by Vivek Sharma. Target: about 1 week.

    2. Double-Blind Evaluation

    Blinded manuscript sent to two independent external reviewers with subject expertise. Target: about 3 weeks, depending on reviewer availability.

    3. Author Revision

    Authors submit point-by-point rebuttal and revised manuscript incorporating reviewer remarks, followed by the editorial decision. Target: 1–2 weeks after reviews arrive.

    4. Acceptance & Publication

    Final editorial decision by Vivek Sharma, typesetting, author proof check, and open-access publication. All timelines on this site are targets and may be longer.

    📜 Journal Policies

    Plagiarism & Originality Policy

    All manuscripts are screened with similarity-check software. Submissions must be original, unpublished and not under review elsewhere. A similarity index above 10% results in return to the authors before peer review. Text recycling, duplicate submission, image manipulation and data fabrication are treated as misconduct and handled following COPE flowcharts.

    Corrections & Retraction Policy

    Honest errors are corrected through a published correction notice linked to the original article. Articles are retracted when there is clear evidence of misconduct, unreliable findings or undisclosed duplicate publication. Retracted articles remain online with a visible retraction notice, following COPE and ICMJE recommendations.

    AI Use Policy

    Authors must disclose any use of generative AI tools in writing, data analysis or figure preparation, naming the tool and its purpose. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy of the content. Reviewers and editors must not upload confidential manuscripts to AI tools.

    Complaints & Appeals

    Authors may appeal an editorial decision by emailing the editorial office within 30 days with a point-by-point justification. Complaints about editorial process, reviewers or published content are acknowledged within 7 days and investigated as per COPE guidance. Appeals are not guaranteed to change a decision.

    Conflicts of Interest & Confidentiality

    Authors, reviewers and editors must declare competing interests. Editors and reviewers handle manuscripts confidentially and recuse themselves from submissions where a conflict exists. Peer-review identities remain blinded.

    Archiving & Preservation

    Published articles are retained in the journal's online archive and the public repository. The journal intends to deposit articles with a long-term preservation service and assign DOIs; the arrangements will be named here once in place.

    🔒 Privacy Policy

    What personal data Pharmionex Journal collects, why, who sees it, and how you can control it. Last updated: 4 October 2026.

    Who we are

    Pharmionex Journal is published by Pharmionex Scientific Publications, India, and edited by Vivek Sharma (Editor-in-Chief & Publisher). We decide why and how the personal data described below is used. We aim to handle it in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it. Contact: pharmionex.journal@gmail.com.

    What we collect

    • When you submit a manuscript (through the submission portal or the Google Form): the corresponding author's name, email address and affiliation; co-author names; the manuscript title, abstract, keywords and files; supplementary files; and your funding, conflict-of-interest, data-availability and ethics-approval statements.
    • When you email us: your email address and whatever you choose to send.
    • If you are invited as a reviewer or editorial board member: your name, email address, affiliation and expertise, and your review comments.
    • On your device: the submission portal saves your unfinished draft and a record of submissions made from that browser in your browser's own storage. This stays on your device until you submit or clear it. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.
    • Hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type under its own privacy terms.

    Please do not include patient-identifiable information in a manuscript unless it is essential and you have consent.

    Why we use it

    • To receive your submission, send you an acknowledgement and a Tracking ID, and contact you about it.
    • To screen the manuscript (including similarity checks), arrange peer review, and reach an editorial decision.
    • To publish accepted articles and keep the journal's records.
    • To handle complaints, appeals and suspected misconduct, and to meet legal obligations.

    We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

    Your consent

    By submitting a manuscript you agree to us using your data for the purposes above. If you list co-authors, you confirm they know their names and affiliations are being shared with us. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing means we may no longer be able to review your manuscript, and it does not undo anything already lawfully done, including publication of an article that has already appeared (see below).

    Who can see your data

    • The editor sees all submission details.
    • Reviewers receive the manuscript without author names, emails or affiliations. Reviewers must keep it confidential.
    • Google provides the tools we use to receive and store submissions (Google Drive, Sheets, Gmail, Forms and Apps Script). Google acts as our service provider, and your data may be stored or processed on its servers outside India.
    • The public sees the title, author names, affiliations, abstract and full text of an article once it is published. Published articles are open access under CC BY 4.0 and are intended to stay available permanently.
    • Others only if the law requires it, or to deal with research misconduct (for example, informing an author's institution).

    Tracking page: anyone who has your Tracking ID can see your manuscript's title, track, status and the corresponding author's name. Keep your Tracking ID private.

    How long we keep it

    Submission records and files are kept while a manuscript is under consideration and afterwards for as long as we reasonably need them for editorial records, complaints and appeals. Published articles and their author information stay in the journal's archive. You can ask us to delete the files and personal details of an unpublished submission.

    Security

    Submissions are sent from your browser to our Google account, and access is limited to the editor. No online system is perfectly secure, so please do not send anything you could not accept being exposed in a breach.

    Your rights

    You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used, to correct or complete it, to erase it (subject to the limits on published articles above), and to withdraw your consent. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights for you in the event of your death or incapacity. To make a request or a complaint, email pharmionex.journal@gmail.com with "Privacy" in the subject line. This address is also our grievance contact; the person responsible is Vivek Sharma. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

    Children

    The journal is meant for researchers and practitioners. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
