🔬 Comprehensive Scope Across All Pharmaceutical Disciplines

Pharmionex Journal welcomes high-quality submissions spanning the entire spectrum of pharmaceutical science and healthcare:

1. Pharmaceutics & Drug Delivery: Lipid nanoparticles (SLN, NLC), liposomes, SNEDDS, polymeric micelles, transdermal, ocular, pulmonary, and targeted anticancer delivery systems.
2. Pharmaceutical Analysis & QA: Stability-indicating HPLC/UPLC, LC-MS/MS, capillary electrophoresis, forced degradation, impurity profiling under ICH Q2(R1) validation.
3. Industrial Pharmacy & GMP: Hard gelatin capsules, solid oral dosage forms, WHO Schedule M compliance, 21 CFR Part 11 computerized validation, cleanroom engineering.
4. Pharmacology & Pharmacokinetics: Preclinical animal models (IAEC), Level A IVIVC correlation, bioequivalence, compartmental PK/PD deconvolution, toxicokinetics.
5. Medicinal Chemistry & Discovery: Computer-aided drug design (CADD), molecular docking, QSAR modeling, synthesis and bio-evaluation of novel therapeutic heterocycles.
6. Pharmacognosy & Phytomedicine: Herbal drug standardization, bioactive phytoconstituents isolation, chromatographic fingerprinting, and phytotherapeutic mechanisms.
7. Clinical Pharmacy & Pharmacovigilance: Adverse drug reactions (ADR) causality assessment, drug interactions, therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), hospital practice.
8. Biopharmaceutics & Biotechnology: Monoclonal antibodies, charge heterogeneity, biosimilars, peptide therapeutics, recombinant protein characterization.

    📖 Instructions for Authors & Manuscript Guidelines

    Comprehensive standards for manuscript preparation, reporting frameworks, reference styles, and ethical requirements, aligned with the norms expected of standard UGC-CARE listed journals.

    🏛️ Publication Standards (UGC-CARE Aligned)

    Pharmionex Journal follows the practices expected of standard, peer-reviewed journals considered for the UGC-CARE list. Manuscripts must meet the following requirements.

    • Peer Review: Every manuscript undergoes double-blind review by at least two independent subject experts before a decision.
    • Originality & Plagiarism: Work must be original and unpublished. Similarity must be below 10% (UGC academic integrity regulations); manuscripts above this are returned.
    • Publication Ethics: COPE and ICMJE standards apply to authorship, conflicts of interest, data integrity, corrections and retractions. Fabrication, falsification, duplicate submission and salami slicing are not accepted.
    • Authorship & Identity: Full names, affiliations, corresponding-author email and ORCID iD are required for each author. Author contributions must be stated.
    • Research Ethics: IAEC / IEC approval, informed consent and CTRI registration (where applicable) must be reported in the manuscript.
    • Transparency: Funding, conflict of interest and data availability statements are mandatory. Use of AI tools must be disclosed; AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
    • Referencing: Consistent Vancouver style, with DOIs. Avoid citations to predatory or unverifiable sources.
    • Open Access & Licence: Articles are published under CC BY 4.0 with no author fees.

    Note: UGC-CARE listing is decided solely by UGC-CARE. These guidelines describe the standards the journal follows and do not state any listing or indexing status.

    🧾 Standard Manuscript Structure

    1. Title page: Title, running title, all authors with affiliations and ORCID iDs, corresponding author email.
    2. Abstract & Keywords: Structured abstract within the limit for the article type; 4 to 6 keywords.
    3. Main text: Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion (or the structure for your article type).
    4. Back matter: Acknowledgements, Author Contributions (CRediT roles), Funding, Conflict of Interest, Ethics Statement, Data Availability.
    5. References: Numbered in order of citation, Vancouver style, with DOIs.
    6. Tables, figures & supplementary files: Each cited in the text with a numbered title and legend.

    📐 Manuscript Formatting

    • File Formats: Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), or LaTeX archive (.zip).
    • Typography: 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial, double-spaced with continuous line numbers.
    • Title Page: Running title, full author names, institutional affiliations, and corresponding author email.
    • Keywords: 4 to 6 terms chosen from Medical Subject Headings (MeSH).
    • Author Details: ORCID iD for every author; contributions stated using CRediT roles.
    • Declarations: Funding, conflict of interest, ethics and data availability statements included in the manuscript.

    🖼️ Figures & Artwork (300 DPI)

    • Resolution: Minimum 300 DPI for photographs and halftones; 600 DPI for line graphs.
    • Formats: TIFF, PNG, EPS, or high-resolution PDF.
    • Labeling: 8–10 pt sans-serif font (Arial/Helvetica); clearly defined axes and units.
    • Chromatograms: Clear baseline, labeled retention times, and solvent peak annotations.

    📚 Reference Citations (Vancouver Style)

    • Citation in Text: Numbered consecutively in order of appearance using square brackets [1] or superscripts.
    • Journal Article Example: Sharma V, Patel RK. Formulation of lipid nanocarriers. Pharmionex J. 2026;1(1):1-16.
    • Pharmacopoeial Reference: United States Pharmacopeia. USP 46–NF 41. Rockville, MD; 2023.
    • DOI Requirement: Include the DOI for each cited reference where available.

    ✅ Pre-Submission 12-Point Checklist

    ☑️ 1. Authorship: All co-authors meet ICMJE criteria and approved final manuscript.
    ☑️ 2. Plagiarism: Similarity index verified < 10% (similarity-check software).
    ☑️ 3. Ethics: IAEC animal approval number or CTRI trial ID specified.
    ☑️ 4. Disclosures: Competing financial interests and funding sources declared.
    ☑️ 5. Blinding: Title page blinded in main manuscript for double-blind peer review.
    ☑️ 6. Artwork: High-resolution figures (≥ 300 DPI) uploaded with clear legends.
    ☑️ 7. References: Formatted in standard Vancouver style with DOIs.
    ☑️ 8. Data Access: Raw chromatographic / spectral datasets available in supplementary files.
    ☑️ 9. ORCID & Contributions: ORCID iD and CRediT author contributions provided for all authors.
    ☑️ 10. Structure: Abstract, keywords, main text, back matter and references follow the standard manuscript structure.
    ☑️ 11. AI Disclosure: Any use of AI tools declared; no AI tool listed as an author.
    ☑️ 12. Journal Quality: All cited sources are verifiable; no citations to predatory journals.

    🔄 Double-Blind Peer Review Workflow

    1. Scope & Similarity Check

    Similarity screening (< 10% threshold) and editorial fit review by Vivek Sharma (Days 1–3).

    2. Double-Blind Evaluation

    Blinded manuscript dispatched to two independent external reviewers with subject expertise (Days 4–18).

    3. Author Revision

    Authors submit point-by-point rebuttal and revised manuscript incorporating reviewer remarks (Days 19–28).

    4. Acceptance & Publication

    Final editorial decision by Vivek Sharma, typesetting, galley proof, and open-access publication.
