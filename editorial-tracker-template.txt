Operating notes
- Keep identity and blinded review files clearly separated.
- Save received files, reviewer reports, author responses and decision letters by manuscript ID in a restricted folder.
- Record only factual process notes and dates; avoid subjective or sensitive personal details.
- Back up records appropriately; define retention and deletion arrangements before inviting submissions.
