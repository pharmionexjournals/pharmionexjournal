Editorial accountability

Concerns, appeals
& corrections.

A clear route for authors, reviewers, readers, and institutions to raise a concern about the editorial process or the published record.

Contact: Email pharmionex.journal@gmail.com. The editorial office aims to acknowledge correspondence within five working days; investigation and decision times depend on the case and are not guaranteed. Include only the information needed to identify and assess the matter. Do not email full manuscript files, patient-identifiable information, or confidential reviewer reports unless the editorial office requests a suitable restricted method.

Appeal an editorial decision

What to include

  1. Use the corresponding-author email and include the manuscript ID, decision date, and decision letter subject.
  2. Identify the specific procedural error or substantive misunderstanding you believe affected the decision.
  3. Respond to the relevant points with concise evidence. An appeal is not a substitute for a new submission or a general disagreement with editorial judgment.
  4. Do not send a revised manuscript unless the editor requests it. Keep the appeal and any new evidence in a restricted folder if the office asks for files.

The journal acknowledges appeals and records them in its private case log. An appeal does not guarantee reconsideration or acceptance. The person who made the original decision should not decide their own appeal.

Independent handling: The journal has not yet named an independent alternate editor. An appeal or concern involving the Editor-in-Chief must be assigned to a qualified, uninvolved person who has agreed to serve before a substantive decision is made.

Complaints and integrity concerns

How the journal assesses concerns

Send the manuscript ID or article link, a short description of the concern, and relevant dates or supporting information. The journal will acknowledge receipt, check conflicts, record the case, and assess it fairly. The editor may request a response from affected parties or refer the matter to an institution or independent adviser. Receipt of a complaint is not a finding of misconduct.

Where the Editor-in-Chief is involved, the concern must be handled by an uninvolved independent person. Confidentiality will be respected as far as practicable, but absolute anonymity or confidentiality cannot be promised where a fair assessment requires disclosure.

Correct the scholarly record

Request a correction or review

For a published article, send the article URL or verified DOI, the exact passage or record that may be wrong, the proposed correction, and any evidence. If the issue concerns authorship, include the position of all affected authors. Do not send confidential participant data.

The journal will assess whether a correction, expression of concern, or retraction is warranted, document its reasoning, and link any notice to the affected article. The original publication history remains identifiable.
