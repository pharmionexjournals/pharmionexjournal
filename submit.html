Author guidelines

Prepare your
manuscript.

Use these requirements to prepare a clear, complete and review-ready manuscript. The journal may update technical requirements as its editorial workflow develops; authors should check this page before each submission.

Submission standard: Manuscripts should be original, clearly written, ethically conducted where applicable, and formatted so that editors and reviewers can evaluate the work efficiently.

01 · Before submission

Submission checklist

01

Originality

The manuscript must be original, not published elsewhere, and not under simultaneous consideration by another journal. All authors should approve the submitted version.

02

Author details

Provide each author's full name, department, institution, city/country, email address and the corresponding author's contact details.

03

Ethics & declarations

Include applicable ethics approval, informed consent, clinical-trial registration, conflicts of interest, funding and author-contribution statements.

04

Files

Submit an editable manuscript file plus separate high-quality figure files where appropriate. Tables should remain editable rather than being pasted as images.

02 · Manuscript format

Formatting requirements

ItemRecommended requirement
File formatMicrosoft Word (.docx) preferred; editable text is required for production.
Page sizeA4, portrait orientation, with approximately 2.54 cm (1 inch) margins on all sides.
FontTimes New Roman, 12 pt for the main manuscript text. Use a consistent font throughout.
Line spacingDouble spacing for the main text and references is recommended for peer review.
AlignmentLeft aligned; avoid full justification and manual spacing used to position text.
ParagraphsUse consistent paragraph spacing; do not insert repeated spaces or tabs for layout.
Page numbersNumber all pages consecutively, preferably in the footer.
Line numbersContinuous line numbering is strongly recommended for original research and review manuscripts.
UnitsUse SI units wherever applicable and report measurements with appropriate units and precision.
AbbreviationsDefine an abbreviation at first use and avoid unnecessary abbreviations in the title and abstract.

03 · Article structure

How to organize the manuscript

01

Title page

Concise title; author names; affiliations; corresponding-author details; and, where applicable, ORCID iDs. Keep the title informative and avoid unnecessary abbreviations.

02

Abstract

Use a concise abstract. For original research, a structured abstract with Background/Aim, Methods, Results and Conclusion is recommended. Aim for about 200–300 words unless the article type requires otherwise.

03

Keywords

Provide 3–6 keywords that accurately describe the work. Use established biomedical terminology where possible.

04

Main text

Original research should generally follow Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion and Conclusion. Use numbered or clearly hierarchical headings.

05

References

Use numbered references in the order cited in the text. A Vancouver-style biomedical reference format is recommended unless the editor specifies another style for a particular article type.

06

Declarations

Include applicable ethics approval, consent, conflicts of interest, funding, author contributions, data availability and acknowledgements before the references.

Typical original research structure

  1. Title page
  2. Abstract
  3. Keywords
  4. Introduction
  5. Materials and Methods
  6. Results
  7. Discussion
  8. Conclusion
  9. Declarations
  10. References
  11. Tables and figure legends

Article-specific structures may differ for reviews, case reports, brief communications, methodological papers and other submissions.

05 · Tables, figures & supplementary files

Artwork and data presentation

T

Tables

Number tables consecutively in order of citation. Give each a concise title and define abbreviations in a note below the table. Do not duplicate all table data in the text.

F

Figures

Number figures consecutively and provide descriptive legends. Use clear labels and readable fonts. Raster images should generally be at least 300 dpi; line art should be supplied at higher resolution where possible.

S

Supplementary files

Clearly name supplementary datasets, methods, checklists, questionnaires or additional figures. Cite every supplementary item in the main manuscript.

06 · References & citations

Reference style

In-text citations: Number references consecutively in the order in which they first appear. Use the same number when a source is cited again.

Journal article example: Author AA, Author BB. Title of article. Abbreviated Journal Title. Year;volume(issue):pages. doi:xx.xxxx/xxxxx.

Book example: Author AA. Book Title. Edition. City: Publisher; Year.

Accuracy: Authors are responsible for checking every reference, DOI, page range and author name. Use DOI links where available.

07 · Research integrity

Required declarations

01

Ethics approval

Human or animal research must state the approving committee/institution and approval or protocol number where applicable. Follow relevant national/institutional requirements.

02

Informed consent

State whether informed consent was obtained when required. For identifiable case material or images, obtain appropriate written consent for publication.

03

Clinical trials

Where applicable, provide the trial registration name and registration number and report the study according to relevant reporting standards.

04

Conflicts & funding

Declare all relevant financial or non-financial competing interests and identify all sources of funding or state that no specific funding was received.

05

Author contributions

Describe each author's contribution using a recognized contributor framework where practical, and confirm that all authors approved the final manuscript.

06

Data availability

State where supporting data can be accessed, or explain why data cannot be publicly shared. Do not disclose confidential participant information.

07

Use of AI tools

Disclose substantive use of generative AI or other AI tools in manuscript preparation where required by the journal's policy. AI tools must not be listed as authors.

08 · Cover letter

What to include

Your cover letter should briefly state the manuscript title, article type, principal contribution, originality status, and confirmation that all authors approve the submission. Disclose any relevant conflicts, prior related publications or preprints, and suggest reviewers only if requested by the journal.

Do not include confidential patient information or unnecessary personal data in the cover letter.

Author confirmation

  • Original work and not simultaneously submitted elsewhere.
  • All listed authors approve the submission.
  • Ethical and regulatory requirements have been addressed.
  • References and figures have been checked.
  • Required declarations are included.

09 · Submission workflow

After you submit

Editorial workflow

  1. Technical and completeness check
  2. Scope and editorial screening
  3. Peer review where applicable
  4. Editorial decision
  5. Revision and author response, when requested
  6. Final acceptance and production
  7. Publication and indexing/archiving processes where applicable

Submission note

Submitting a manuscript does not guarantee peer review or publication. Editorial decisions are based on scope, completeness, methodological quality, originality, ethical compliance and the journal's review process.

Zero-cost launch workflow: submissions are received by email and tracked manually. Read the email submission workflow before sending files.

Ready to submit?

Send your manuscript

This static website has no upload portal or manuscript tracker. Submit an editable manuscript, separate title page, figures/supplements, applicable reporting checklist, and cover letter by email. Use the subject: New submission — [short title] — [article type]. Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal or identifiable patient information.
