Editorial stages

  1. Received and initial completeness check
  2. Editorial screening
  3. Reviewer selection and invitation
  4. Peer review, when applicable
  5. Revision requested or decision issued
  6. Acceptance and production checks
  7. Published, if accepted and production is complete

Displayed stage descriptions are general updates, not a promise of a decision date. Formal decisions and reviewer comments are communicated by the editorial office.
