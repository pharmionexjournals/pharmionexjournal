PHARMIONEX RESEARCH JOURNAL — REVIEWER GUIDELINES

Thank you for considering a review. Accept only if the topic fits your expertise, you can meet the deadline, and no conflict prevents an impartial assessment. If uncertain about a conflict, contact the handling editor before viewing or assessing the manuscript.

CONFIDENTIALITY
- Treat the manuscript, files, invitation, and review as confidential. Do not forward, quote, publish, or discuss them outside the editorial process.
- Do not contact the authors directly.
- Do not upload manuscript text, figures, or reviewer materials to generative-AI tools or other third-party services.
- Do not include identifiable patient or participant information in your report. Tell the editor promptly if you see unnecessary identifiers.
- The private report link is unique to your assignment. Do not forward it. Contact the editor if it is lost or appears to have been shared.

CONFLICTS AND WITHDRAWAL
Disclose financial, personal, professional, institutional, collaborative, or other interests that could reasonably affect impartiality. If a conflict arises after accepting, pause and tell the editor. The editor will decide whether you should continue, be replaced, or provide only limited advice. A disclosed conflict must be assessed before your report is used.

ASSESSMENT
Give a fair, evidence-based assessment of the question, methods, analysis, interpretation, reporting, ethical safeguards, references, and relevance. Separate essential concerns from optional suggestions. Explain why a point matters and indicate where possible what would address it. Be respectful and specific; do not make discriminatory, personal, or unsupported remarks.

CONFIDENTIAL REPORT FORM
The individual assignment link asks for a recommendation, summary, major and minor comments, methods/statistics, ethics/reporting concerns, confidential editor-only comments, and a conflict declaration. Put comments intended for the authors in the relevant comments fields. Use the editor-only field only for matters that must not be shared with authors. The editorial office receives the full report; authors may receive an anonymized version of author-facing comments.

RECOMMENDATION CATEGORIES
- Accept: no substantive changes remain; production checks may still be required.
- Minor revision: limited changes or clarifications are needed.
- Major revision: substantial work is needed and may require another review.
- Reject: the work is unsuitable or serious concerns cannot be resolved through revision.

Reviewers advise; editors make and document the decision. A recommendation does not guarantee a particular outcome. Contact the handling editor if you need an extension or must decline. The journal aims to acknowledge editorial correspondence within five working days; this is not a promised review or decision deadline.
