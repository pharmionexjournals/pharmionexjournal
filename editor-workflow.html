Launch operations · online intake

A simple, clear
editorial workflow.

Pharmionex uses a Google Form linked to an automated private editorial tracker. Editors continue to make all scholarly and ethics decisions. Email is available for access problems and correspondence.

Before accepting manuscripts: Confirm the named editor and inbox backup, test the live form from a non-editor account, adopt the published policies, verify restricted file access, and establish private backups and a retention schedule. The public website does not store or transmit manuscript files.

For authors

Submit through the intake form

  1. Download the author checklist and manuscript template from Author Resources.
  2. Prepare an editable manuscript, separate title page for blinded review, figures/supplements, applicable reporting checklist, and cover letter.
  3. Create a Drive folder for the submission, share it directly with pharmionex.journal@gmail.com, and paste the folder URL in the form. Do not enable public link access.
  4. Complete the manuscript intake form. The system emails a receipt and manuscript ID; receipt is not peer-review acceptance. The receipt email includes the manuscript ID and private status-check code; enter both on the status page to check the current stage.
  5. Do not include unnecessary sensitive or identifiable patient information. If Drive sharing or form access fails, email the editorial office for help.

For editors

Manual handling steps

  1. Confirm the automated receipt and manuscript ID in the tracker; check completeness and scope. Keep author identity separate from the blinded review file.
  2. Screen ethics, declarations, reporting checklist, originality concerns, and conflicts. Record dates and decisions in a private tracker.
  3. Invite qualified reviewers individually. Send a blinded manuscript and deadline; ask reviewers to declare conflicts and confirm confidentiality. Use BCC or separate messages to protect reviewer identities.
  4. Make and document the editorial decision. Send the decision and consolidated comments to the corresponding author without exposing reviewer identity unless the review model explicitly allows it.
  5. For revisions, keep original and revised files, a point-by-point response, and the decision history together under the manuscript ID.
  6. When an editor changes the decision to Accepted, automation creates a private production-checklist row and emails the editorial inbox. Acceptance is not public release.
  7. Complete the release gate: verify authors and affiliations, ORCIDs where supplied, funding, data availability, ethics/consent, trial registration, CRediT contributions, references and reference metadata, dates, licence, metadata exports, accessibility, preservation copies, and a genuine second-person review. The private tracker checks required fields and links before public listing. Set Approved to publish = YES only after all checks pass.
  8. Publish the article HTML page and PDF to the journal website. Include the verified article history, references, declarations, licence, and DOI only if it has actually been registered. Record live HTTPS links, issue metadata, and publication date in the private checklist.
  9. After the live page and PDF are verified, set Public listing approved = YES, then set Publication status = Published. The current-issue and archive article lists update automatically, and the corresponding author receives a published status notice. Assign a DOI only after an actual registration arrangement is in place.

Reviewer report route: After the private operations upgrade is activated, marking a reviewer invitation READY sends that reviewer an individual report link. Their report is stored in the private Reviewer Reports tab and alerts the editor. Check the conflict declaration before relying on the report; a disclosed conflict requires independent assessment. The private Dashboard counts overdue reports, revision deadlines, accepted/production work, published articles, and open editorial cases.

Appeals and integrity cases: A staff editor logs each appeal, complaint, correction request, or research-integrity concern in the private Editorial Cases tab, checks conflicts, acknowledges receipt, assigns an uninvolved editor, and records the next action and due date. See the public publisher/governance and preservation record for confirmed and pending details. See the author and reader concerns route. A concern involving the Editor-in-Chief must wait for an independent alternate editor; that appointment is currently pending.

Weekly automatic backups cover the private tracker spreadsheet only, not manuscript folders or website files. Keep access to manuscripts limited to the handling editor and assigned reviewers. Use account security features and a separate, access-restricted folder. Define a retention/deletion schedule and honor applicable privacy and records requirements. Email and consumer cloud services have limitations; do not describe this workflow as a secure submission portal.
