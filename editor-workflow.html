Launch operations · email-based

A simple, clear
editorial workflow.

Pharmionex uses email and manually maintained records during its zero-cost launch phase. This is a small-journal workflow, not an online submission portal or automated tracking system.

Before accepting manuscripts: Name an editor responsible for the inbox, confirm the email account is accessible and monitored, adopt the published policies, and establish access-restricted storage for submissions and the editorial log. The public website does not store or transmit manuscript files.

For authors

Submit by email

  1. Download the author checklist and manuscript template from Author ResourcesEditorial Workflow.
  2. Prepare an editable manuscript, separate title page (for blinded review), figures/supplements, applicable reporting checklist, and cover letter.
  3. Email the files to pharmionex.journal@gmail.com with subject New submission — [short title] — [article type].
  4. Do not email identifiable patient information unless necessary and appropriately consented. Do not include sensitive personal data in the cover letter. Authors should retain their own submission copy.
  5. The editorial office should acknowledge receipt, assign a manuscript ID, and record each status change. Do not send confidential reviewer reports to other authors.

For editors

Manual handling steps

  1. Check completeness and scope; acknowledge receipt and provide an ID such as PX-2026-001. Keep the ID separate from author identity for blinded review.
  2. Screen ethics, declarations, reporting checklist, originality concerns, and conflicts. Record dates and decisions in a private tracker.
  3. Invite qualified reviewers individually. Send a blinded manuscript and deadline; ask reviewers to declare conflicts and confirm confidentiality. Use BCC or separate messages to protect reviewer identities.
  4. Make and document the editorial decision. Send the decision and consolidated comments to the corresponding author without exposing reviewer identity unless the review model explicitly allows it.
  5. For revisions, keep original and revised files, a point-by-point response, and the decision history together under the manuscript ID.
  6. On acceptance, obtain final author approval, verify metadata and rights, prepare the final HTML/PDF, and publish a permanent article page. Assign a DOI only after an actual registration arrangement is in place.

Keep access to manuscripts limited to the handling editor and assigned reviewers. Use account security features and a separate, access-restricted folder. Define a retention/deletion schedule and honor applicable privacy and records requirements. Email and consumer cloud services have limitations; do not describe this workflow as a secure submission portal.
