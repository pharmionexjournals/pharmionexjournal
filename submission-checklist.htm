PHARMIONEX RESEARCH JOURNAL — SUBMISSION CHECKLIST

Before submission
[ ] This work is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. Related preprints, abstracts, or prior reports are disclosed.
[ ] All authors meet authorship criteria, approved this version, and agree to submission.
[ ] A corresponding author is identified with current contact details; affiliations and ORCID iDs are accurate where available.
[ ] The article type, title page, abstract, keywords, main text, references, tables, figures, legends, and supplements are complete.
[ ] Continuous line and page numbers are included where possible; the main manuscript is editable.
[ ] The applicable reporting checklist (e.g., CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE, CARE, STARD, ARRIVE, COREQ/SRQR, SQUIRE or CHEERS) is included where relevant.
[ ] Ethics approval or exemption, committee and identifier are reported for applicable human/animal research.
[ ] Consent is reported where applicable; written publication consent is secured for identifiable case details/images.
[ ] Trial registration, registry identifier, and registration timing are reported where applicable.
[ ] Funding, sponsor role, competing interests, author contributions, acknowledgements, data availability, and AI use are declared (or explicitly stated as none/not applicable).
[ ] Permissions for reused figures, tables, instruments, and third-party material are obtained.
[ ] Figures are legible, numbered, cited in order, and supplied as separate files when appropriate.
[ ] References, links, and DOI values have been checked against source records.
[ ] Cover letter is included and all required files are attached.

This checklist supports completeness and does not guarantee peer review or acceptance. Confirm current journal instructions before submitting.
