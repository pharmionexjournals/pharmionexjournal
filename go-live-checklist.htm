PHARMIONEX JOURNAL — GO-LIVE CHECKLIST

Do not accept live manuscripts until the editorial office completes and records the checks below. A public website and a live form alone do not establish an operational journal.

A. Account and access
[ ] Confirm the journal Gmail account has a named primary owner and backup with recovery options and strong account security.
[ ] Confirm the private tracker and manuscript folders are accessible only to authorized people; remove unnecessary access.
[ ] Agree who monitors new submissions, inbox coverage, and expected acknowledgement cadence.
[ ] Establish a private backup and recovery routine for the tracker, accepted article files, and website source.
[ ] Adopt a written record-retention/deletion schedule and apply relevant institutional and privacy requirements.

B. Live intake acceptance test (perform from a non-editor account)
[ ] Confirm an external author can open and submit the form without an unintended sign-in restriction.
[ ] Confirm receipt email, editor alert, tracker row, and sequential manuscript ID.
[ ] Confirm the author can provide a folder link and that only the journal account/editorial team can access the files.
[ ] Change a test record's status and confirm the expected author notification.
[ ] Test reviewer invitation using an internal test address; confirm READY sends one invitation and does not duplicate it.
[ ] Test the unique reviewer report link, conflict declaration, private Reviewer Reports entry, editor alert, duplicate submission handling, and no public access to tracker data.
[ ] Confirm reminders are understandable and capped; inspect Apps Script execution history for errors.
[ ] Open the private Dashboard and confirm counts. Create a manual backup, confirm its restricted folder access, and document how to restore the tracker. Confirm the weekly trigger is installed.
[ ] Test the author status checker with a correct ID/code and an incorrect code; confirm it reveals only the broad stage and update date.
[ ] Delete the dummy record, test files, and any test messages that should not remain in the live record.

C. Governance and policy decisions
[ ] Confirm the named Editor-in-Chief and handling editor(s), with permission to publish their accurate profiles.
[ ] Confirm the public institutional affiliation is accurate and does not imply the institution publishes, sponsors, or endorses the journal without written authorization.
[ ] Appoint an independent alternate editor for conflicts involving the Editor-in-Chief and appeals.
[ ] Name a contact for research-integrity concerns and a backup for inbox coverage.
[ ] Record the independent alternate editor's written agreement and authority to handle EiC conflicts and appeals before the first such case arises.
[ ] Approve the review model, conflict procedure, appeal procedure, complaint pathway, and reviewer confidentiality instructions.
[ ] Approve and publish the fee policy, publication frequency, copyright/licence terms, publisher details, and retention policy. Keep unapproved items marked pending.
[ ] Confirm the policy framework and operational practices before inviting submissions; do not claim external endorsements or indexing.

D. First-article release checklist
[ ] Record final editorial acceptance and all author approvals.
[ ] Verify title, author order, affiliations, ORCIDs, abstract, keywords, dates, declarations, references, funding, and article type.
[ ] Check ethics approvals/consent, trial registration and applicable reporting checklist.
[ ] Complete plagiarism/image-integrity and reference/link review using available editorial procedures; document the editor's assessment.
[ ] Confirm the publication agreement and reuse licence applicable to this article.
[ ] Prepare accessible HTML and a final PDF; verify they match and include references and declarations.
[ ] Create and publish a stable article HTML page and final PDF to the site. Enter the live HTTPS article/full-text/PDF URLs and issue metadata in the private Publication Checklist.
[ ] Complete downloads/article-accessibility-checklist.txt for HTML and PDF; check screen-reader heading order, visible keyboard focus, figure alternatives, responsive layout, and selectable/tagged PDF text. This checklist is not itself a WCAG audit.
[ ] Publish only identifiers and indexing/preservation claims that have actually been assigned or activated.
[ ] Have a second authorized person review the completed production checklist before release. After verifying the live files, set Public listing approved = YES, then Publication status = Published. Confirm the article appears on Current Issue and Archives and the author receives the published status notice.

E. Governance assignments that cannot be automated
[ ] Name a second authorized editor / independent alternate.
[ ] Name a backup owner for the editorial inbox and account recovery.
[ ] Approve and date a retention/deletion schedule for submissions, reviewer data, case records, and backups; assign who carries it out.
[ ] Verify all board member names, credentials, affiliations, consent to serve, and conflicts before publishing appointments.

Record completion date, responsible person, evidence/link, and any exception for each checked item. Keep this completed checklist private with the editorial records. Do not upload a completed checklist to the public site.
